JP Cotton — Store Manager Guide

Simple, plain-English instructions for everyday store operations. No technical knowledge required!
×

How to Process and Ship a Customer Order

Step 1: Check New Orders

Click Orders in the sidebar. Any new purchase will appear at the top marked as Pending.

Step 2: View Customer Phone & Address

Click the blue "View" button next to the order. You will see the customer's full name, 10-digit mobile number, pincode, and exact delivery address, plus whether they chose Cash on Delivery (COD) or online payment.

Step 3: Update Order Status

Use the status dropdown in the table:
• Processing: You are currently packing the item.
• Shipped: You have handed the parcel to the courier (Delhivery, Blue Dart, Speed Post, etc.).
• Delivered: The customer received the parcel.

How to Add a New Garment to Your Store

Step 1: Open the Product Form

Click Products in the sidebar, then click the "+ Add Product" button at the top right.

Step 2: Enter Basic Details

• Name: e.g. Indigo Floral Hand-Block Kurta
• Category: Select Women, Men, or Kids.
• Subcategory: e.g. Co-Ord Sets, Kurtas, or Suit Sets.
• Selling Price (₹): The amount the customer pays (GST inclusive).
• Original MRP (₹): If on sale, enter the higher price to show a discount strike-through.

Step 3: Upload Product Photos

Click Browse Photos and select one or more images from your phone or computer. The first photo becomes the main showcase image!

How to Manage Stock When Items Sell

1-Click Adjustment Buttons

Click Live Stock & Inventory in the sidebar. Every garment has green and red + and - buttons. If you sell 1 piece in-store, just click - once!

Low Stock Warnings

Whenever any item drops to 10 pieces or fewer, it automatically flags orange under "Low Stock Alerts" so you know to reorder from your weavers.

How to Change Homepage Hero Slides & Story Photos

Homepage Hero Slides

Click Hero Slides in the sidebar. Click + Add New Slide to upload a landscape photograph (1920x1080px). Old slides can be deleted with the red Delete button.

Our Story Atelier Photography

To update the artisan block printing photos on Our Story, replace the files in assets/ or upload them directly to your assistant!

How to Create Festival & Promo Coupons

Creating a Promo Code

Click Marketing → + Create Discount Code. Type a code (e.g. DIWALI15) and discount percentage (e.g. 15). Customers can enter this during checkout for an instant discount!
